My name is Vanja, I am a 3rd year Computer science student at TU Graz.

Welcome to my GitHub - a graveyard of deeply useful and inspiring projects. Some of my greatest hits include Wordle - an already pre-existing game I dumbed down just enough for me to be able to produce a somewhat playable terminal version of - and Snake. Snake, if you havent heard, is an extremely complicated strategy game, where the player takes upon the role of a hungry snake (surprise!) and through complicated manoevers and fine motor skills rarely observed in everyday life, feeds the bottomless pit its stomach appears to be. Obviously, in my version, you can only play it in terminal (retro style!) and even then it only sometimes works since I haven't actually finished it.

Anyways, (un)professional and spectacular commentary aside, here you can see some of the projects I wasn't too scared to make public. Some of them were mandatory university assignments that I so shamelessly just uploaded for my own personal gain, but some projects are products of my own curiosity. You'll come to find that the latter is very easily distinguishable, since the complexity factor decreases by quite a bit.

That being said, however, I DO actually have hopes and plans (as well as prayers) for these mini-projects. Even though I am astutely aware of the saying "shoot for the stars," I must admit, I don't even have a space-suit. That is why I am starting with seemingly simple projects. ("Seeminlgy" being a strong word, here.) Additionally, I am also aware of ANOTHER saying: "Slow and steady wins the race." This, I can actually implement in my daily life as I am already one of the two integral adjectives - slow. A rather scintillating description of who I am, but hey, at least I was honest. I could've framed it as: "I prefer a systematic approach to all my projects, focusing on honing all the necessary skills by dissecting each large project into smaller, more comprehensible components, and treating them as vital excersize."

Anyways, take a quick scroll through my page. If you find anything interesting or noteworthy, PLEASE hit me up. Anything is well received - criticism, questions, orders, suggestions, gossip... anything.

If my page proves to be yet another bountiless harvest, I am deeply sorry, but at least you can probably laugh about it later. Or compare yourself to me, in which case you'll probably feel great.
